How a tensor flows through the model.

Eight stops on the journey from raw token IDs to a probability distribution over the next word. Each step explained in detail — what changes, why it changes, and what stays the same.

Throughout: B = batch size (32 sequences in parallel), T = sequence length (256 tokens), C = embedding dimension (384 numbers per token), V = vocabulary size (4,096 possible tokens). These are the exact shapes your trained model uses.
