About these vectors: the embeddings shown here are hand-designed for teaching purposes — each word has a 12-dimensional vector with axes I labeled by hand (animal, action, emotion, etc.). In a real trained model, the dimensions are not labeled and don't correspond cleanly to human concepts. The model invents whatever 384 dimensions are useful for predicting the next token. Some end up roughly aligned with concepts we recognize; most don't. The principle is the same: similar words end up with similar vectors because they appear in similar contexts during training.
